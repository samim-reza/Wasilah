# Play Store listing

Copy for the Google Play Console. Character limits are Google's and are enforced
at submission.

---

## App name (30 max)

```
Wasilah — One Ayah a Day
```

24 characters. The subtitle carries the promise, because the name alone means
nothing to someone who does not read Arabic.

## Short description (80 max)

```
Read one ayah a day. A small Quran habit that lasts, with gentle reminders.
```

74 characters. Leads with the action, names the scope ("one ayah"), and signals
the tone ("gentle") — the three things that decide whether the right person
installs it.

## Full description (4000 max)

```
Wasilah is built around one idea: reading a single ayah every day should be so
easy there is never a reason not to.

Most Quran apps are readers. Wasilah is a reader with a habit built around it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ONE AYAH IS ENOUGH

Your daily minimum is one ayah. That is what keeps your streak — not ten pages,
not thirty minutes. On the days you have nothing left to give, one ayah still
counts as a day you showed up.

You can set a larger goal for the good days. The minimum stays where it is.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TODAY'S AYAH

Open the app and one ayah is waiting, with its translation and recitation. The
same ayah for everyone, all day, so it means something to share.

Read it, mark it, and your day is done.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A STREAK THAT SURVIVES REAL LIFE

Your streak is counted in your own timezone. It survives travelling, daylight
saving, a flat battery and a week without signal. Read offline and it syncs when
you are back — your streak is never lost to a bad connection.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

REMINDERS THAT LEAVE YOU ALONE

Most apps remind you because they can. Wasilah tries not to.

• Nothing is sent once you have read for the day
• Quiet hours are on by default
• At most a couple of reminders a day, and you choose the cap
• Ignore a few and it quietly sends fewer
• Every category can be switched off on its own

Optional reminders around prayer times, calculated on your device.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE QURAN, PROPERLY

• Full Quran with translations in English and Bengali
• A typeface designed for Quranic script, not a general-purpose Arabic font
• Recitation by well-known reciters, with background playback
• Tafsir, word-by-word, and search
• Bookmarks, collections, and private notes
• Adjustable text size for comfortable long reading

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

START WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

Read everything and build a streak with no sign-up. Create an account later only
if you want it synced across devices — and your existing streak comes with you.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PRIVACY

• No ads, ever
• No advertising identifiers
• Analytics are off unless you turn them on, and never record what you read
• Your notes are private to you
• Location is optional, only for prayer times, and only ever approximate

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MILESTONES, NOT POINTS

Wasilah marks what you have read. It does not score you, rank you against
anyone, or suggest that a number reflects anything beyond activity in an app.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Quran data provided by Quran Foundation.

Wasilah is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or an official product of Quran.com or the Quran Foundation.
```
