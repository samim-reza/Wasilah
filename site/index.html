One ayah. Every day.

A free Quran reading app for Android.

Most reading plans fail the same way: they ask for more than a bad day can give. Wasilah separates the two numbers that usually get confused — the goal you are aiming for, and the minimum that keeps your streak alive. Set the minimum to a single ayah and a hard day costs you thirty seconds instead of your progress.

What it does

  • The full Quran with translation, tafsir and recitation
  • A streak built on your daily minimum, not your goal
  • Bookmarks, collections and private notes
  • Reminders that back off when you are already reading, and stay quiet at night
  • Prayer times, calculated on your device
  • Offline reading of anything you have already opened
  • Works fully without an account

What it does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or crash reporting unless you switch them on
  • No record of which verses you read, anywhere
  • No precise location — coordinates are rounded to about 11 km before storage
  • No microphone or camera access; the permissions are not even declared

Quran content

Quran text, translations, tafsir and recitations come from the Quran Foundation APIs, requested through our own server so that no reading of yours is ever attributable to your device. Nothing is bundled with the app and nothing is redistributed.
